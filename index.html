TACTICAL BRIEFING

HOW TO PLAY

✦

1. COLLECT ENERGY SHARDS

Navigate with the Joystick (or WASD / Arrow Keys). Collect all golden shards to unlock the emerald Exit Portal.

⧖

2. THE SIGNATURE ECHO SYSTEM

After a 2.4s delay, a violet Ghost repeats your exact path. Touching it damages you, but you can BAIT enemies into chasing your ghost while you escape!

⚡

3. PHASE DASH

Tap DASH (or press SPACE) for a burst of speed with invulnerability frames to slip through danger.

🌀

4. KINETIC PULSE & ESCAPE

Tap PULSE (or press E) to stun and push back nearby enemies with an EMP shockwave, then enter the green Vortex to advance!
